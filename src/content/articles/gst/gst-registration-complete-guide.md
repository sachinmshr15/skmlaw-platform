# GST Registration Complete Guide

Welcome to the SKM Laws Knowledge Centre.

This is the article about GST Regsitration.

## Who should register?

In India, GST registration is mandatory when a business's annual aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh for exclusive suppliers of goods (in most states) and ₹20 lakh for service providers or mixed suppliers. Lower thresholds of ₹20 lakh (goods) and ₹10 lakh (services) apply in special category/hill states.

## Documents Required

Mandatory Documents for All Businesses
-PAN Card: Photo or scan of the owner/business PAN.
-Aadhaar Card: Photo or scan of the owner/partners/directors.
-Photographs: Passport-sized photos of promoters, partners, or primary authorized signatory.
-Bank Proof: Cancelled cheque, bank statement, or passbook copy showing the account name, number, and IFSC.
-Proof of Principal Place of Business: You must provide proof of address for your business location depending on ownership:
-Owned Premises: Property tax receipt, municipal khata copy, or electricity bill.
-Rented/Leased Premises: Valid rent agreement or lease deed, along with an utility bill (like electricity or water) in the owner's name.-Shared/Free Premises: Consent letter from the property owner, plus a copy of their utility bill.
-Additional Documents by Business EntitySole Proprietorship: No extra entity documents needed beyond personal IDs.
-Partnership Firm: Partnership deed and registration certificate (if registered).
-LLP / Private Limited Company: Certificate of Incorporation, LLPIN/CIN, and Board Resolution or Letter of Authorization appointing the primary signatory.
